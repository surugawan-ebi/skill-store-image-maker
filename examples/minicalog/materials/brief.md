# Minicalog Store Image Brief

## Product
- Title: Minicalog
- App type: Tomica collection management app
- Store targets: App Store and Google Play concept exports
- Orientation: portrait screenshots, plus Google Play feature graphic
- Primary language: Japanese

## Positioning
- One-sentence pitch: 写真つきでトミカの所有状況・欲しいもの・タグ分類を整理するコレクション管理アプリ。
- Core loop: 一覧で探す -> 詳細を見る -> 購入済み/ほしいを記録 -> タグや保存フィルタで再表示する。
- Most visible strengths in provided captures:
  - 写真グリッドでコレクションを見渡せる
  - 購入済み/ほしいを詳細画面で切り替えられる
  - タグ/カテゴリで大量データを探せる
  - よく使う絞り込みを保存できる

## Assumptions
- The original production run used four user-provided phone screenshots. This public documentation snapshot includes three approved captures; the settings capture is intentionally excluded because it contains production-only operational identifiers.
- The provided captures include real status bars and live times. For final store upload, cleaner simulator captures with consistent time/battery are recommended.
- No unsupported claims such as ranking, download counts, official endorsement, or limited-time promotions are used.
