# QA Notes

This file records the QA result for the original full Minicalog production set. The public case-study snapshot includes three App Store images, one Google Play feature graphic, and one Instagram feed image; the additional outputs named below are intentionally omitted to keep the example compact.

## Current Draft QA

- Source captures are preserved separately.
- Drafts use real app screenshots as the main visual.
- Overlay copy is short and does not claim unsupported facts.
- No download CTA, ranking claim, price, sale, testimonial, or store badge is included.
- Google Play text area is kept under the practical 20% guidance.
- Instagram ad creatives use real app screenshots and do not include unsupported claims.
- Instagram feed, square, and Story/Reel exports keep key screenshot UI visible without stretching.
- The public example omits the original settings/filter capture because it contains production-only operational identifiers.
- App Store URL, Android preparation note, and CTA are kept in ad copy/setup rather than crowded into the image.

## Remaining Before Final Store Upload

- Capture clean simulator screenshots with a consistent status bar, or crop/status-normalize the provided captures.
- Decide final store targets:
  - App Store only
  - Google Play only
  - both
- Confirm whether English localization is needed.
- Confirm whether the app name should appear as `Minicalog`, `ミニカログ`, or both.
- Run final visual review on phone-size thumbnails.
